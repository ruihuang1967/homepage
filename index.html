Rui (Ray) Huang

Senior Research Engineer, AI Solutions, RBC Borealis AI

I am a Senior Research Engineer at RBC Borealis, where I work on building AI solutions for Royal Bank of Canada (RBC), specifically for risk and credit management. My expertise includes AI/ML-based designs for representation, prediction, and reconstruction in large and complex systems.

Before joining RBC Borealis, I worked at Samsung Research America, focusing on delivering AI/ML solutions for Samsung's commercial platform, such as mobile services and devices.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from The University of British Columbia in 2023, and my M.Sc. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2018.
